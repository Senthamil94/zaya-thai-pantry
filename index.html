Our story เรื่องราวของเรา

Authentic taste
in every dish.

The owners of Zaya Thai Pantry decided to make their dream of building a world-class Thai restaurant come true quickly after they took their vows.

Don't let the word “Pantry” fool you.

Although casual, the owners spent months going over every detail of the restaurant in pursuit of an experience both warm and rich, but above all welcoming. Sprouting from generations of restaurant owners, PJ seemed to be preparing for the opening of Zaya her whole life.
